HOW WE WORK From first meeting to long-term partnership. We move with conviction but never with haste. Our diligence process is designed to build mutual understanding — we want founders to evaluate us as much as we evaluate the opportunity. Our network spans community banks, regional institutions, and money-center banks, giving portfolio companies direct access to early customers from day one.
